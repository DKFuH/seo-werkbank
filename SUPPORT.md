# Support

SEO-Werkbank is maintained in spare time. Where to go:

- **How do I use it?** [Schnellstart](docs/SCHNELLSTART.md), [Benutzerhandbuch](docs/BENUTZERHANDBUCH.md) and the help page in the app (`/help`).
- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/DKFuH/seo-werkbank/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** [open an issue](https://github.com/DKFuH/seo-werkbank/issues/new/choose) with version, steps to reproduce and logs.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Known gaps:** check [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) and [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md) first.
